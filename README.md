# Pocket Ledger

A simple expense tracker that installs on your phone like an app and works offline.

- Date-wise expenses with categories (petrol, engine oil, bike, household, milk, fee, misc and your own)
- Cash in hand plus any number of bank accounts, with a live balance
- Money in and transfers between accounts (for example an ATM withdrawal)
- Monthly totals, category breakdown and CSV export

All data stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to any server.

## Publish it (GitHub Pages)

1. Settings, then Pages.
2. Source: Deploy from a branch. Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
3. After a minute or two the site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/pocket-ledger/`.

## Install on a phone

- Android (Chrome): menu, then Install app.
- iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Open the app from its home screen icon and export a CSV now and then as a backup.
